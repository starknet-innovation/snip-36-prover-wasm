What is—and isn’t—proved

The proof binds the CoinFlip contract’s result to this round’s player, locked seed, and choice. Settlement reads the attached proof facts on-chain. Wallet keys stay in your wallet; the browser’s public executor cannot spend your tokens.

This is an experimental Sepolia demonstration. A future block hash is not a certified randomness beacon. This demo does not establish production-grade gambling fairness. After revealing, settlement requires a compatible proof-bearing wallet or another account submitting the exported proof; it is permissionless and always pays the recorded player.
