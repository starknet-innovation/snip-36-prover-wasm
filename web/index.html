WHAT THIS DEMONSTRATES

One flip. An execution you can prove.

The upstream contract computes pedersen(seed, player) mod 2 and emits [player, seed, choice, outcome, matched] as a virtual L2→L1 message. The browser validates the account and state, executes that exact contract, and can prove the resulting public output.

This recorded round has a known outcome. It does not implement the backend's commit/reveal service, deposits, or bank settlement, and does not claim unpredictable randomness. A locally generated proof is not an on-chain acceptance receipt.
